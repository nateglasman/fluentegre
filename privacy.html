Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

What We Collect

fluenteGRE is designed to respect your privacy. We do not require account creation, and we do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

Local Storage

Your study progress (word ratings, mastery scores, and session history) is stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and general geographic region. This data is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify individual users.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. For more information about how Google uses data, see Google's Privacy & Terms.

Cookies

fluenteGRE itself does not set cookies. However, third-party services such as Google AdSense may set cookies for advertising purposes. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Third-Party Links

This site does not link to external services for data collection. Any external links are informational and subject to those sites' own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

fluenteGRE is an educational tool intended for graduate school applicants. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at hello@fluentegre.me.
